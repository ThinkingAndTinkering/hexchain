HOW TO PLAY

—
Draw a path through adjacent tiles of the same color by touching and dragging.
3+
Chains of 3 or more clear and score. Longer chains score way more: a 6-chain is worth 10x a 3-chain!
↓
After clearing, tiles settle down and new tiles fill the gaps.
❄
In Classic, tiles that sit too long will freeze. A 6-chain thaws & resets all tiles of that color! A 7-chain resets all tiles!
↻
Stuck? You get 2 reshuffles per game. Use them wisely — when they're gone, a deadlock ends the game!
🏆
Classic: Unlimited moves, but tiles freeze. Sprint: 45s timer — long chains (4+) earn bonus time! A 4+ chain wipes all tiles of that color!
